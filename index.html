Project Overview

At BreakFree, we believe that every child deserves the chance to thrive. Our project focuses on empowering street children in Adigrat, Tigray, Ethiopia, through a holistic approach including education, skills, creativity, and community support.

Our Sustainable Model

Children learn to monetize skills like art, content creation, and crafts.

Health services and family programs for holistic community well-being.

📈 BreakFree’s MEAL Framework

To ensure our work drives meaningful change, BreakFree follows a structured Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning (MEAL) framework. It helps us track progress, uphold transparency, and continuously learn from the communities we serve.

Our MEAL approach includes inclusive data practices, ethical safeguards, adaptive learning cycles, and feedback from participants to strengthen impact.
