Project Overview

At BreakFree, we believe that every child deserves the chance to thrive. Our project focuses on empowering street children in Adigrat, Tigray, Ethiopia, through a holistic approach including education, skills, creativity, and community support.

Our Sustainable Model

Education & Skill Development

Foundational literacy, digital skills, and creative arts for lifelong success.

Income-Generating Activities

Children learn to monetize skills like art, content creation, and crafts.

Health & Family Support

Health services and family programs for holistic community well-being.

Innovative Use of AI

AI enhances education, creativity, and personalized health monitoring.
