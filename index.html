Project Overview

At "BreakFree," we believe that every child deserves the chance to thrive. Our project focuses on empowering street children in Adigrat, Tigray, Ethiopia, through a holistic approach that includes education, skill development, creative expression, and community support. By addressing both immediate needs and long-term opportunities, we aim to break the cycle of exploitation and poverty, creating lasting change for the children and the community.

Our Sustainable Model

Education & Skill Development

Our educational programs provide foundational literacy, digital literacy, and creative arts training, equipping children with the skills they need to succeed in life.

Income-Generating Activities

We empower children by teaching them valuable skills that can be monetized, such as art creation, content development, and craft production, fostering self-sufficiency.

Health & Family Support

Our health services ensure the physical well-being of the children, while our family support programs aim to strengthen the overall environment in which they live.

Innovative Use of AI

We leverage AI to enhance learning, creativity, and health monitoring, ensuring that every child receives personalized and effective support.

Leadership & Community Impact

🌟 Leadership

From founding a Girls’ Club in high school to serving as Vice President of my school parliament, I’ve led initiatives aimed at uplifting and empowering others from an early age. My leadership journey culminated in founding BreakFree—a grassroots social innovation platform that empowers disadvantaged children and women through education, digital skills, and tech-enabled solutions. As founder and project lead, I’ve developed strategies, mobilized volunteers, built partnerships, and led product development from idea to deployment.

🤝 Volunteering

As an AIESECer and student mentor, I’ve contributed to programs promoting digital literacy, youth empowerment, and mental health. My efforts were recognized by the Women’s Affairs Office in Adigrat for mobilizing girls toward education and resilience—especially in post-conflict contexts.

🌍 Community Service

Rooted in my personal experience of childhood hardship in Adigrat and shaped by the conflict in Tigray, my work through BreakFree addresses systemic challenges using technology and compassion. Here are a few projects I’ve led:

  • NourishKids – A full-stack platform addressing childhood malnutrition (SDG 2), built using Next.js, Node.js, Firebase, and AI tools. Features include:
    • 🔍 Smart Meal Planner powered by machine learning
    • 📊 Impact Assessment Dashboard
    • 🧮 BMI Analyzer for nutritional tracking
    🔗 Live Demo
  • NourishKidsAgri – A farming assistant platform using Streamlit, Python, and AI for crop and irrigation guidance. Features:
    • 🌱 Crop recommendation system
    • 💧 Irrigation management assistant
    • 🤖 AI chatbot with offline support for rural areas
    🔗 Live Demo
  • Souls for Success – A mental wellness and reintegration initiative supporting young women in vulnerable communities. Developed under the Millennium Fellowship (MCN2024) at USIU-Africa, in collaboration with 22 fellows.
  • Tech-Enhanced Learning Spaces (TLT Project) – In partnership with the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program, VS Management & Consulting, and USIU-Africa, our team Transformative Architects renovated five classrooms at Church Road Educational Centre. We equipped the school with modern PCs and provided IT training that reached over 100 students—fostering a tech-empowered culture and safe learning environment.

    My role included:

    • 💬 Mentoring students in life skills and confidence-building
    • 🖥️ Teaching computer literacy aligned with school curriculum
    • 🎨 Coordinating classroom painting and creative design

    Today, students enjoy bright, tech-equipped classrooms that inspire digital growth and academic confidence.
